# Finance & Admin

> [!quote] You can love coaching and still lose the business if you don't watch the money. This is how Next Level stays healthy enough to fund the [[Vision|next location]].

## SOPs to build in this area

### 1. Money in (revenue)
- Billing runs through **Square + PushPress** — document the flow.
- Failed-payment / dunning process (recover revenue that's slipping)
- Daily/weekly reconciliation (ties to [[Facility & Equipment|closing]])
- Revenue lands in **QuickBooks** ("Next Level Performance Facility, Corp")

### 2. Money out (expenses)
- Approval process for spending _[set a threshold that needs your sign-off]_
- Recurring bills list and owners
- Vendor list & contacts

### 3. Payroll
- Pay schedule and process
- Coach compensation model _[document]_
- Contractor vs. employee handling

### 4. Bookkeeping & the monthly close
- Weekly: categorize transactions, review cash
- Monthly close checklist → produces P&L + Balance Sheet
- Who does it (you / bookkeeper / accountant) and by when

### 5. Reporting & review
- **Weekly:** cash + [[Key Metrics|key numbers]] pulse
- **Monthly:** P&L, margin, vs. [[Annual Goals & OKRs|goals]]
- **Live:** [CEO Dashboard](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f564d4fd-3562-4080-9dfb-25dc02084e1b)

### 6. Admin & compliance
- Insurance (liability — critical for a training facility), renewals
- Waivers & member agreements (signed, stored, current)
- Licenses/permits and renewal dates
- Key docs and where they live
