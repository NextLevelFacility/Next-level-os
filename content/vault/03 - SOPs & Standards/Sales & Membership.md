# Sales & Membership

> [!quote] Great coaching that no one buys helps no one. Sales is how the mission reaches more athletes. We sell with integrity — because we genuinely believe the answer for a serious athlete is Next Level.

## SOPs to build in this area

### 1. Lead handling (speed wins)
- Where leads come from (see [[Marketing & Content]]) and where they land.
- **Respond within _[e.g. 15 min]_** — speed-to-lead is the #1 conversion lever.
- Qualifying questions: is this athlete/family a fit? (see [[Positioning & Promise]])

### 2. The facility tour / consult
> [!example] Tour framework
> 1. **Connect** — learn the athlete's sport, goals, frustrations.
> 2. **Diagnose** — where are they now vs. where they want to be?
> 3. **Show the path** — how Next Level's system closes that gap.
> 4. **Prove it** — the [[The Next Level Standard|standard]], results, the environment.
> 5. **Invite** — clear next step (trial or sign-up), no pressure-cheese.

### 3. Trial / intro offer
- Structure of the trial (what they get, how long)
- The experience must be *exceptional* — this is the audition.
- Follow-up sequence after the trial.

### 4. Closing & sign-up
- Membership options and pricing _[document your tiers]_
- Handling common objections (price, time, "let me think about it")
- The sign-up process in **Square / PushPress**, step by step.

### 5. Onboarding a new member
- Hand-off from sales → coaching (nothing falls through the cracks)
- Kicks off the [[Member Experience & Front Desk|first-30-days experience]]
- Assessment booked (see [[Coaching & Training Delivery]])

### 6. Referrals & upsells
- How and when to ask for referrals (happy members, after a win)
- Referral incentive _[define]_
- Upgrade paths (more sessions, add-ons, camps)
