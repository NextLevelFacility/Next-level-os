# Facility & Equipment

> [!quote] The space *is* the brand before anyone says a word. A clean, dialed-in facility says "these people are serious." A messy one undoes great coaching.

## SOPs to build in this area

### 1. Opening procedure
- [ ] Unlock, lights, music, temperature
- [ ] Equipment check — everything safe and in place
- [ ] Floors and surfaces clean
- [ ] Front desk / systems ready
- [ ] Walk the [[The Next Level Standard|standard]] before first member arrives

### 2. Closing procedure
- [ ] Equipment racked and wiped
- [ ] Floors cleaned
- [ ] Trash, laundry, restock
- [ ] Systems logged out, cash/day reconciled (see [[Finance & Admin]])
- [ ] Lock up, secure, alarm

### 3. Cleaning schedule
- **Daily:** high-touch surfaces, floors, restrooms, equipment wipe-down
- **Weekly:** deep clean _[assign]_
- **Monthly:** _[define]_
- Who owns each — no ambiguity.

### 4. Equipment maintenance & inventory
- Full equipment inventory list _[build this]_
- Inspection schedule (safety-critical gear first)
- Repair/replace process and vendor contacts
- Reorder points for consumables

> [!note] Airtable connection
> Your Airtable "Facilities management" base (Facilities / Maintenance / Locations tables) can hold the live inventory & maintenance log. It's currently the **default starter template** — replace the sample data with real Next Level equipment.

### 5. Safety & injury response
- First-aid kit locations and contents
- **Injury response protocol** — step by step, who does what, when to call whom
- Incident report form and where it's filed
- Emergency contacts and exits posted
