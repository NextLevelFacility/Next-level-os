# Coaching & Training Delivery

> [!quote] This is the product. Everything else supports what happens in a session. Document it so every coach — current and future — delivers the same excellence.

## SOPs to build in this area
Each becomes its own note (use [[SOP Template]]) as you flesh them out.

### 1. Athlete assessment & onboarding
- How we assess a new athlete (movement screen, baseline testing, goals convo)
- What we measure and record _[define your metrics: speed, strength, mobility, sport-specific]_
- How we set goals and communicate them to athlete + parent
- ✅ *Outcome: every athlete starts with a baseline and a plan.*

### 2. Session structure (the anatomy of a Next Level session)
> [!example] The standard template — customize
> 1. **Welcome & tone-set** (by name, energy up) — _2 min_
> 2. **Dynamic warm-up / activation** — _10 min_
> 3. **Main work** (strength / speed / skill per the program) — _30 min_
> 4. **Finisher / conditioning** — _10 min_
> 5. **Cooldown + what-got-better recap** — _5 min_
> - Coach:athlete ratio standard: _[define]_
> - Every session references the athlete's plan — no random workouts.

### 3. Programming & progression
- How programs are built per goal / age / sport / season
- How and when we progress load and difficulty
- Where programs live and how coaches access them _[tool/binder]_

### 4. Progress tracking & re-testing
- Cadence of re-assessment (e.g. every _[8–12]_ weeks)
- How results get shown to athlete + parent (the "proof of progress")
- Links to [[Retention Playbook]] — visible progress = retention

### 5. Coaching standards & conduct
- Tone: high standard, high support. Never sarcasm or humiliation.
- Safety first — spotting, form, injury response (see [[Facility & Equipment]])
- What great coaching looks like vs. "just supervising" (see [[The Next Level Standard]])

### 6. Group & class delivery *(if applicable)*
- Managing energy and individual attention in a group
- Scaling exercises for mixed abilities
