# Marketing & Content

> [!quote] Marketing isn't spend — it's the mission, made visible. Every post is a chance to show a family what's possible for their athlete.

## SOPs to build in this area

### 1. Brand consistency
- Logo, colors, fonts, voice — the look & sound of Next Level _[document or link brand kit]_
- The **voice**: confident, motivating, expert, warm. Never cheesy or desperate.
- The brand check before anything publishes (see [[The Next Level Standard]] → "The brand").

### 2. Content system
> [!example] The content engine
> - **Pillars:** ① athlete results/transformations ② coaching value/tips ③ culture/behind-the-scenes ④ the [[Brand Story|story]] & the why.
> - **Cadence:** _[define — e.g. X posts/week per platform]_
> - **Workflow:** capture → edit → caption → schedule → post → engage.
> - Where content lives and who owns each step.

### 3. Social media management
- Platforms and priority (IG/TikTok primary for youth sports)
- Posting SOP and schedule
- **Comment & DM response** — reply like the [[Member Experience & Front Desk|standard]] demands; DMs are leads.
- *(You have an [[AI Employee]]-style spec available — see the ai-employee-builder skill — to automate posting + comment replies for @nextlevelfacility.)*

### 4. Lead generation & capture
- Where leads come from and how each is captured (form, DM, walk-in)
- Every lead lands somewhere tracked and gets the [[Sales & Membership|fast response]].
- Founding-member / promo offers and how to run them.

### 5. Reviews & reputation
- How and when to ask for Google reviews (after a win / milestone)
- Responding to reviews — good and bad
- Referral program promotion

### 6. Local presence
- Relationships with schools, teams, clubs
- Events, camps, community days
- Partnerships and cross-promotion

> Consistency beats cleverness. Once the voice, look, and 4 pillars are written down, anyone (or an AI employee) can produce on-brand content without you approving every post.
