# Member Experience & Front Desk

> [!quote] The coaching is why they come. The experience is why they stay — and why they tell other parents. This is culture made operational.

## SOPs to build in this area

### 1. Arrival & check-in
- Greeted **by name** within seconds — nobody walks in unseen.
- Check-in process (app / desk / PushPress) documented step-by-step.
- The vibe on arrival: what a member should feel in the first 30 seconds.

### 2. Communication standards
- Response-time standard: reply within _[e.g. 4 business hours]_.
- Channels we use and for what (text, email, app, in-person).
- Tone: confident, warm, expert. See [[The Next Level Standard]].
- **Parent communication cadence** — proactive progress updates so families never wonder how their kid is doing.

### 3. New member first-30-days experience
- Welcome sequence (message, what to expect, first-session prep)
- Check-in touchpoints at day 1, 7, 30
- The goal: by day 30 they're certain they made the right choice. Ties to [[Member Journey]].

### 4. Handling complaints & problems
> [!example] The recovery framework
> 1. **Listen fully** — don't defend, understand.
> 2. **Own it** — "You're right, that's not our standard."
> 3. **Fix it fast** — solve, then over-deliver a little.
> 4. **Follow up** — close the loop personally.
> 5. **Log it** — recurring complaints reveal a broken system to fix.

### 5. Cancellations & win-backs
- The cancellation conversation (understand the real reason — see [[Retention Playbook]])
- Save offers / pauses where appropriate
- Exit gracefully; the door stays open. Log the reason.

### 6. Front desk daily duties
- Opening front-of-house checklist
- During-day standards (tidy, attentive, present)
- Closing front-of-house checklist
