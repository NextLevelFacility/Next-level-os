# Expansion Playbook

> [!quote] The goal of this page: turn "open a new location" from a terrifying gamble into a checklist. When opening #2 feels like following a recipe, you're ready to scale.

## Ownership models (decide which — or a mix)
| Model | You get | You give up | Best when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Company-owned** | Full control + full profit | All the capital & risk | Early sites, protecting the brand |
| **Licensing** | Fees for your system/brand, less capital | Some control | System is documented, partners are strong |
| **Franchising** | Fastest scale, other people's capital | Most control, heavy legal/support burden | Playbook is bulletproof & brand is proven |
| **Partnership / JV** | Local operator + shared risk | Shared profit & decisions | Great operator, new market |

## The pre-opening checklist (repeat for every location)

### 1. Market & site
- [ ] Confirm demand: enough target athletes/families in the area?
- [ ] Site meets spec (size, ceiling height, parking, zoning, lease terms)
- [ ] Competitive landscape mapped
- [ ] Unit economics model says it works ([[Key Metrics]])

### 2. Money
- [ ] Buildout budget + 6-month operating runway secured
- [ ] Break-even math done — # members to profitability
- [ ] Funding source confirmed (cash flow, loan, investor, franchisee)

### 3. Build & brand
- [ ] Buildout to the [[The Next Level Standard|standard]] (not "good enough")
- [ ] Equipment list ordered (see [[Facility & Equipment]])
- [ ] Signage, branding, space feel = unmistakably Next Level

### 4. People
- [ ] Location leader hired & trained *at an existing site first*
- [ ] Coaches hired via [[Hiring & Onboarding]]
- [ ] Every role in [[Org Chart & Roles]] covered

### 5. Systems
- [ ] All [[SOP Index|SOPs]] in place and localized
- [ ] Software/tools set up (booking, payments — Square/PushPress, comms)
- [ ] Metrics dashboard live from day 1

### 6. Launch
- [ ] Pre-launch marketing & founding-member offer (see [[Marketing & Content]])
- [ ] Grand opening event planned
- [ ] Waitlist / pre-sales before doors open

## The "does the system travel?" test
Location #2 is really a test of your **systems**, not your hustle. If opening it required Gabriel to personally do everything, the system failed — fix the system before #3.

## Post-launch review
After each opening, run a debrief: what the playbook got right, what it missed. **Update this page every time.** The playbook should get better with each location.
