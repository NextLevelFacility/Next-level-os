# Growth Roadmap

> [!quote] The dream is ONE massive flagship that owns this market ([[Vision]]). So we don't grow by adding buildings — we grow by making this one deeper, bigger, and more unbeatable, stage by stage.

## The stages to flagship dominance

### Stage 0 — Prove it (a great, profitable facility) ✅ largely here
**Goal:** A facility that is genuinely excellent and reliably profitable.
- Consistent [[Key Metrics|profitability]] (2026 YTD: ~24.7% net margin — strong).
- Members happy, retaining, and referring.
- The [[The Next Level Standard|standard]] is real and visible.

### Stage 1 — Systematize (get it out of Gabriel's head) ⬅️ **WE ARE HERE**
**Goal:** The business runs on documented systems, not memory.
- [[SOP Index|SOPs]] written for every core function.
- [[Org Chart & Roles|Roles]] defined — Gabriel stops doing every job.
- [[Hiring & Onboarding|Onboarding]] that turns a new coach into a Next Level coach.
- Numbers you trust ([[Key Metrics]] — close the Square/PushPress member-count gap).
- ✅ *Exit test: you could take a 2-week vacation and nothing breaks.*

### Stage 2 — Build the bench
**Goal:** Leaders who run the floor so Gabriel can build the flagship.
- A #2 (GM / head coach) who owns daily operations.
- Coaches developed through our own system, training the next ones.
- ✅ *Exit test: someone other than Gabriel runs the facility to standard.*

### Stage 3 — Deepen the flagship
**Goal:** More under one roof — become the destination.
- Expand **programs**: more sports, more levels, camps, teams, add-ons ([[Programs & Offerings]]).
- Expand **space & equipment** as demand proves it ([[Facility & Equipment]]).
- Expand **the team**: the best coaches in the region work here.
- ✅ *Exit test: there's no serious athlete in the area we can't serve.*

### Stage 4 — Own the market
**Goal:** Next Level = the obvious and only answer.
- The majority of new members come from referrals and reputation.
- Partnerships with local schools, clubs, and teams run through us.
- The brand is strong enough that athletes travel to train here.
- Financial position funds constant reinvestment — the gap keeps widening.

### Someday / maybe — beyond the flagship
Only from total strength: license the system, open #2, or sell big. The [[Expansion Playbook]] stays ready on the shelf. **Not before the flagship is undeniable.**

## Right now
**Current stage: 1 — Systematize.** This vault IS the Stage 1 work.
**Biggest blocker:** the business still lives mostly in Gabriel's head → keep turning it into [[SOP Index|SOPs]], one process at a time.
