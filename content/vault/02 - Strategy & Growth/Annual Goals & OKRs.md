# Annual Goals & OKRs

> [!quote] The vision is the mountain; goals are this year's climb. Keep it to 3–4 objectives. More than that and you're not focused — you're just busy.

## How this works
- **Objective** = a bold, qualitative goal for the year.
- **Key Results** = 2–4 numbers that prove you hit it.
- Review **monthly**, grade **quarterly**.
