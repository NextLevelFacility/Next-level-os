# Member Journey

> [!quote] Map every step from "never heard of us" to "tells everyone about us." Each stage is a chance to delight — or to lose them. Design it on purpose.

## The journey, stage by stage

### 1. Stranger → Lead
- They discover us ([[Marketing & Content]]) and raise a hand.
- **Win condition:** captured and responded to *fast*.

### 2. Lead → Trial
- Tour / consult → trial ([[Sales & Membership]]).
- **Win condition:** they experience the [[The Next Level Standard|standard]] first-hand.

### 3. Trial → Member
- Assessment, plan, sign-up.
- **Win condition:** they feel *seen* and shown a real path.

### 4. New Member (first 30 days) — *the make-or-break window*
- Onboarding, early wins, day 1/7/30 touchpoints ([[Member Experience & Front Desk]]).
- **Win condition:** by day 30, "this was 100% the right decision."

### 5. Engaged Member
- Consistent training, visible progress, belonging ([[Culture Code]]).
- Progress re-tests shown to athlete + parent ([[Coaching & Training Delivery]]).
- **Win condition:** results they can feel and see.

### 6. Raving Fan
- They refer friends, leave reviews, rep the brand.
- **Win condition:** we *ask* at peak-happiness moments ([[Retention Playbook]]).

### 7. At-Risk / Departing
- Attendance drops, engagement fades — catch it early.
- Win-back or graceful exit ([[Retention Playbook]]).

## The map
```
Stranger → Lead → Trial → Member → New (30d) → Engaged → Raving Fan
                                         ↘ At-Risk ↗ (catch & save)
```

## Moments that matter most
> [!tip] Obsess over these three
> 1. **First session** — the audition. Must be exceptional.
> 2. **First 30 days** — decides retention more than anything else.
> 3. **First visible result** — turns a customer into a believer.
