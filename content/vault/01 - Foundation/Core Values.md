# Core Values

> [!quote] Beliefs are what we think. Values are how we behave. These are the words we hire by, coach by, promote by, and fire by. If a value isn't something you'd actually fire someone for violating, it's not a value — it's a poster.

## The values (draft — aim for 5)

### 1. Standard over comfort
We do it right, not easy. We hold the bar high even when it's uncomfortable — for athletes, for coaches, for ourselves. See [[The Next Level Standard]].

### 2. Own it
No excuses, no "not my job." If you see it, you own it — the mess, the mistake, the member who looks lost. Extreme ownership from the founder to the newest coach.

### 3. Athlete first
Every decision starts with: *what's best for the athlete and their family?* Revenue follows trust; it never comes before it.

### 4. Get 1% better
Grow or die. We are students of the craft — coaches who train, systems that improve, a company that's better this quarter than last. Complacency is the only failure.

### 5. Win together
We're a team, not a collection of stars. We hand off cleanly, cover each other, celebrate each other, and protect the culture. See [[Culture Code]].

---

## How we use these values
- **Hiring:** Every interview scores the candidate against these 5. A brilliant coach who fails "Win together" is a no. See [[Hiring & Onboarding]].
- **Reviews:** Team members are reviewed on values, not just output.
- **Recognition:** Call out value-driven behavior publicly and often.
- **Decisions:** When stuck, ask "which option best honors our values?"
