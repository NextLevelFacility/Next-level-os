# The Next Level Standard

> [!quote] This is the bar. It's what "good" means at Next Level, written down so it doesn't live only in Gabriel's head. Every location, every coach, every day — this is the line nothing drops below.

## The principle
Excellence isn't one big thing. It's a **stack of small things done right, every time.** A member never notices a single detail — they *feel* all of them at once. That feeling is the brand.

## The standard, by area

### The space
- Floors clean, equipment racked, nothing broken or "we'll fix it later."
- Music, lighting, temperature set intentionally — the room should feel like *game day energy*.
- Walls tell the story: goals, wins, standards visible.

### The coaching
- Every session has a **plan** — warm-up, main work, finisher. No winging it.
- Coaches know athletes' **names, goals, and where they are** in their journey.
- Correct with care: high standard, high support. Never sarcasm, never humiliation.
- Every athlete leaves knowing **what got better and what's next.**

### The member experience
- Greeted by name within seconds of walking in.
- No one stands around lost — someone owns every member's experience.
- Follow-up that shows we noticed them (see [[Member Experience & Front Desk]]).

### The communication
- Reply to members/parents within _[set your standard — e.g. 4 business hours]_.
- Proactive updates on progress — parents never wonder how their kid is doing.
- Tone: confident, warm, professional. We are the expert they trust.

### The brand
- Every post, email, and sign looks and sounds like Next Level (see [[Marketing & Content]]).
- We never publish something "good enough." It represents everyone here.

## The test
Before anything ships — a session, a post, a new hire's first shift, a new location — ask:

> **"Is this Next Level, or is this just fine?"**

If it's just fine, it doesn't go out. That's the whole standard.
