# Culture Code

> [!quote] Culture is what happens when you're not in the room. It's the real product — the reason members stay for years and coaches build careers here. It doesn't happen by accident; you build it on purpose, every day.

## What culture means at Next Level
The **environment** is the thing that changes people ([[Core Beliefs|Belief #2]]). We are ruthlessly intentional about how it feels to be here — for athletes, families, and staff.

## How the culture feels
- **High standard, high support.** We push hard *because* we care, never to tear down.
- **Energy.** The room has a pulse. People leave more alive than they came in.
- **Belonging.** Every member and coach feels like part of something, not a transaction.
- **Pride.** We're proud of the work, the space, and each other — and it shows.

## How we protect it
- **Hire and fire by [[Core Values|values]].** One tolerated bad attitude taxes everyone.
- **Leaders model it.** Culture is set by the worst behavior the leader tolerates.
- **Recognize it out loud.** Catch people living the values; celebrate it publicly.
- **Rituals.** _[Define yours: team huddles, wins-of-the-week, milestone celebrations, athlete shout-outs.]_

## Team rituals *(draft — pick a few and commit)*
- Pre-shift huddle: today's focus + one standard to nail
- Weekly team wins & lessons
- Athlete milestone celebrations (PRs, transformations) — publicly
- Quarterly team gathering that isn't about work

## The culture test for growth
> [!warning] The #1 risk when you scale
> Culture is easy in one room you're standing in. The hard part is keeping it identical in a building you're not in. That's why it must be *written* ([[Core Beliefs]], [[Core Values]], this page) and *lived by leaders* you develop — not vibes only you can produce. Protecting culture across locations is the real test of the [[Vision]].
