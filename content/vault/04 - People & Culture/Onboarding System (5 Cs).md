# Onboarding System — The 5 C's

> [!nl] Onboarding starts the moment they say yes
> Not on Day 1. From offer accepted to Day 90, every new hire follows the same written path. This is how a coach hired in Ocala delivers the same Next Level as one hired in Miami.

Built on Leila Hormozi's 5 C's. Every new hire gets a [[30-Day Plan Template|30-Day Plan]] and a [[Hiring & Onboarding|hiring record]].

## 1. Compliance — the paperwork (before Day 1)
The minimum. Get it done before they walk in so Day 1 is about the work.
- [ ] Offer letter signed, pay and schedule confirmed
- [ ] NDA + non-solicit signed
- [ ] W-4 / I-9 or contractor agreement
- [ ] Payroll set up in QuickBooks
- [ ] Logins created: PushPress, GoHighLevel, ADP coach PIN, email, Slack/WhatsApp group
- [ ] Vault access + "read these first" list sent
- [ ] Next Level gear in their size waiting on Day 1

## 2. Clarity — the 30/60/90 problems to solve
A new hire is hired to **solve problems**, not fill hours. Before Day 1, write their plan with [[30-Day Plan Template]]:
- **Day 30:** learn the system, pass the standards check, run the role with supervision
- **Day 60:** run the role unsupervised, own their scorecard
- **Day 90:** improve something — one SOP rewritten, one number moved
Every plan names the **three problems** they're here to solve and the scorecard they'll be judged on (see their page in [[People Index]]).

## 3. Culture — how Next Level thinks
Read and watch, in this order, before end of Week 1:
1. [[Core Beliefs]] — all eight, and what each looks like on the floor
2. [[The Next Level Standard]]
3. [[Culture Code]] + [[Brand Story]]
4. The Book of Next Level
5. Simon's coaching methodology videos (coaches)
6. Recorded sessions and past team meetings, so they hear how we talk
Then a 20-minute conversation with Gabriel: "Which belief is hardest for you? Which is easiest?"

## 4. Connection — a buddy, not a boss
- Assign a **buddy** who is *not* their manager on Day 1
- Shadow every seat for at least one shift in the first two weeks: front desk, concessions, a coaching session, a sales evaluation
- Week 2: they shadow Vittorio or Simon on one evaluation
- They see how their work hits everyone else's

## 5. Check-back — twice a day for two weeks
- **Weeks 1–2:** two 15-minute check-ins daily (morning: plan, afternoon: what got stuck)
- **Weeks 3–4:** one check-in daily
- **Day 30, 60, 90:** formal review against the plan, logged on their person page
- Question every time: "What did you need today that you didn't have?" → answer becomes an SOP
