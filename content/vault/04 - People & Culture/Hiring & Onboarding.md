# Hiring & Onboarding

> [!quote] Your business will never outgrow the quality of your people. Hire for [[Core Values|values]], train the skill. A skilled jerk poisons the culture faster than a great one builds it.

## Who we hire
- **Values first.** Score every candidate against the 5 [[Core Values]]. A "no" on values is a no, period.
- **Character over credentials.** We can teach our system; we can't teach who someone is.
- **Coachable.** People who want to [[Core Values|get 1% better]] fit here.

## Hiring process (SOP)
1. **Define the seat** — pull it from [[Org Chart & Roles]]; know what success looks like.
2. **Source** — where great candidates come from _[referrals, local, socials]_.
3. **Screen** — quick call: energy, values signals, logistics.
4. **Interview** — structured, scored on values + skill. Same questions every time.
5. **Audition** — have coaches run/shadow a real session. Watch them with athletes.
6. **Reference & decide** — trust the pattern, not the charm.
7. **Offer** — clear comp, expectations, and the [[Vision|why]].

## Onboarding: turning a hire into a Next Level coach
> [!example] The first 2 weeks
> - **Day 1:** [[Brand Story|the why]], [[Core Beliefs|beliefs]], [[Core Values|values]], the [[The Next Level Standard|standard]]. Culture before tasks.
> - **Week 1:** shadow sessions, learn the [[Coaching & Training Delivery|session structure]] and systems, read the relevant [[SOP Index|SOPs]].
> - **Week 2:** run sessions co-piloted, get feedback against the standard.
> - **Sign-off:** they can deliver a session to standard, mostly unassisted.

## The vault *is* the training system
> [!tip] This is the payoff of everything else here
> A new coach should be handed this vault and get *most* of the way to "Next Level coach" from it. The more complete your [[SOP Index|SOPs]], the faster and more consistent onboarding gets — and the closer you are to opening [[Expansion Playbook|location #2]].
