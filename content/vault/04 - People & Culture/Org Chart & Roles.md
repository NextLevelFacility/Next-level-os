# Org Chart & Roles

> [!quote] You scale by replacing yourself, one role at a time. This page defines every seat in the business — even the ones you currently sit in. Naming the seat is the first step to filling it.

## The principle: seats, not people
Design the org as if it already had 3 locations. Every function is a **seat** with an owner. Right now Gabriel may sit in many seats — that's fine, the point is to *see* them so they can be handed off.

## Core seats

### Leadership
- **CEO / Owner (Gabriel)** — vision, growth, [[Expansion Playbook|expansion]], culture, the big calls.
- **Operations Lead / GM** *(next hire?)* — runs the daily floor so the CEO can build. Key to [[Growth Roadmap|Stage 2]].

### Coaching
- **Head Coach** — owns the [[Coaching & Training Delivery|coaching standard]], develops coaches, quality control.
- **Coaches** — deliver sessions to the [[The Next Level Standard|standard]].

### Member-facing
- **Front Desk / Member Experience** — [[Member Experience & Front Desk|arrival, comms, vibe]].
- **Sales / Membership** — [[Sales & Membership|tours, trials, closing]].

### Behind the scenes
- **Marketing / Content** — [[Marketing & Content|brand & leads]] (can be human + [[AI Employee]]).
- **Finance / Admin** — [[Finance & Admin|books, payroll, compliance]] (often outsourced early).
